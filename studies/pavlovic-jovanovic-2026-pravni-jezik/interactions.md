# Interaction with existing project evidence

## Existing bureaucratic-register guidance

`SKILL.md` already warns that verbosity, complexity, vagueness, stereotypy, impersonality, generalization and nominalization form a contextual bureaucratic cluster rather than automatic errors.

This article strengthens that architecture by adding a crucial boundary: in legal Serbian, some of the same surface features are functional resources for precision, generality, reference stability and procedural abstraction.

**Decision:** enrich the existing model; do not create a competing “legal-style reviewer”.

## Existing `editing`

The library is `PROJECT`. This source adds:
- register-first evaluation;
- positive vs negative redundancy;
- controlled indeterminacy;
- legal economy;
- term repetition and intertextuality.

**Decision:** model-level editing guidance only.

## Existing `native_usage`

The article is not a broad native-usage corpus. It can provide register-specific evidence but should not determine general Serbian preferences.

## Existing `serbian_norm`

No direct promotion. Historical material and secondary claims are insufficient for hard gates.

## Existing `ai_style`

No connection to authorship detection. Legal formality must not become an AI signal.

## Interaction with Panić Kavgić 2019

The two sources reinforce the same architectural principle from different directions:

- Panić Kavgić: interpersonal context determines whether directness/markers are appropriate.
- Pavlović Jovanović: institutional register determines whether nominalization, passive, repetition and abstraction are appropriate.

Together they support **context before surface-pattern cleanup**.
