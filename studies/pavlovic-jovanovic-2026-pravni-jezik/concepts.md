# Reusable source model

## 1. Legal Serbian is a specialized functional register

Legal language is treated as part of an administrative-legal functional style with distinct substyles. Legal/legislative text therefore cannot be judged by the same brevity, conversationality or stylistic expectations as ordinary explanatory prose.

**Project consequence:** register detection precedes simplification.

## 2. Precision and indeterminacy can coexist

Legal text needs enough precision to be applicable, but some generality/indeterminacy is functional because rules must cover classes of cases.

**Project consequence:** vague-looking abstraction is not automatically defective when it performs legal generalization.

## 3. Redundancy has positive and negative forms

Repetition and paraphrase can support clarity, continuity and unambiguous reference. Accumulated long expressions and syntactic bulk can instead become negative redundancy.

**Project consequence:** “remove repetition” is not a legal-register rule. Evaluate function and cost.

## 4. Economy is also part of legal style

The source explicitly discusses devices for economy alongside redundancy: compression, omission of recoverable elements, general principles, standardized phrases, noun phrases and nominalization.

**Project consequence:** nominalization is not inherently verbose; in legal drafting it may compact recurring legal relations.

## 5. Nominalization, passive and impersonality are register resources

The article discusses nominalization, impersonal expressions and passive constructions both as means of abstraction/ideological distancing and as ordinary legal-language mechanisms.

**Project consequence:** these forms need discourse-level interpretation; word-form blacklists are invalid.

## 6. Intertextuality is structural

Legal documents refer internally and to other acts. Repetition of terms may be necessary to maintain stable reference.

**Project consequence:** a humanizer must preserve legal cross-reference structure and terminology even when it sounds repetitive.

## 7. Bulky syntax can be characteristic without being desirable

The source notes decomposed predicates, multiword expressions, repeated conjunctions and long complex sentences, but also distinguishes these from awkward overaccumulation and norm violations.

**Project consequence:** register-specific frequency does not turn every occurrence into a flaw; equally, “legal register” does not excuse unreadable or ungrammatical construction.

## 8. Legal genres differ internally

Legislative, business, diplomatic, certification and correspondence substyles have different pragmatic realizations.

**Project consequence:** do not treat “bureaucratic/legal” as one flat style.
