# Current Serbian norm audit

## Result

**No modern Serbian `NORM` rule is promoted from this article.**

Reasons:

1. The paper is primarily a review of legal language and functional stylistics.
2. Many relevant claims are explicitly attributed to earlier works.
3. Historical legal examples are source-period evidence.
4. Statements about awkward syntax or norm violations are not accompanied by a reusable deterministic modern-norm test.
