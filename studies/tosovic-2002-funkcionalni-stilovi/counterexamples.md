# Counterexamples and anti-rules

1. **A repeated template is not automatically AI-like.**  
   A standardized administrative form can repeat identical section structure because rapid, reliable decoding is the point.

2. **A cliché is not automatically bad style.**  
   A recurring journalistic or administrative formula can be functionally conventional; the question is whether it serves the genre or merely adds ballast.

3. **A long sentence is not automatically non-human.**  
   Scientific writing may need a complex sentence to express hierarchy, causality, conditions or definition precisely.

4. **A short sentence is not automatically clearer.**  
   Splitting scientific or legal relations can obscure logical dependency.

5. **Fragments are not automatically errors.**  
   In dialogue/chat, `Važi. Sutra.` can be a complete communicative exchange despite fragmentary syntax.

6. **Oral does not equal conversational.**  
   A conference talk or TV news item is spoken but belongs to scientific/publicistic style, not conversational style.

7. **Written does not equal formal.**  
   A private chat is written yet may be conversational in function.

8. **Expressiveness is not automatically inappropriate.**  
   Publicistic and literary genres can deliberately use rhetoric, imagery, rhythm or emotional force.

9. **Objectivity does not require deleting every first-person form.**  
   Scientific genres vary; textbooks, methods sections and explanatory prose can use inclusive forms or authorial stance.

10. **Administrative standardization is not a license for opacity.**  
    Formulaic structure may be necessary, while needless nominal chains or malformed syntax can still be editorial problems.

11. **Register mixing is not automatically inconsistency.**  
    A public explainer may intentionally move between technical explanation and conversational examples.

12. **Stylistic norm is not language norm.**  
    A form can be grammatically standard yet register-mismatched, or stylistically unusual yet fully grammatical.

13. **Creative markedness is not an AI signal.**  
    Repetition, unusual word order, parcellation and metaphors can be intentional literary devices.

14. **Economy is not a universal optimization target.**  
    Redundancy can be necessary when the reader needs reliable decoding, explicit reference or repeated orientation.

15. **2002 source evidence is not automatically current Serbian usage.**  
    Historical/cross-Yugoslav examples must not be turned into modern hard rules without validation.
