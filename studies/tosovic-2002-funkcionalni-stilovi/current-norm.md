## Important boundary

The book itself gives the project a reason **not** to collapse stylistic appropriateness into grammatical correctness: stylistic and standard-language norms have different obligatoriness.

Any future hard claim about morphology, syntax, government, spelling or punctuation must be checked independently against current Serbian institutional/normative evidence.
