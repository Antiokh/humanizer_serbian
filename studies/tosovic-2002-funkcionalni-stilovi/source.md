## Scope and period

The book is a broad functional-stylistics monograph. Its main analytical body covers:

- theory of stylistics, style and functional style;
- style as information;
- literary-artistic style;
- publicistic style;
- scientific style;
- administrative style;
- conversational style;
- conclusion;
- extensive bibliography, summaries and indexes.

The source was published in 2002 and draws on Serbian, Serbo-Croatian/Yugoslav, Croatian, Bosnian, Russian, Czech/Slovak and other stylistic traditions and examples. It is therefore highly relevant for functional-style architecture but **not** a stand-alone authority for current Serbian standard-language norm.
