# Reusable source model

## 1. Directness and politeness are separate dimensions

The source rejects the shortcut `more indirect = more polite`. Directness can be socially acceptable and natural, while indirectness can be excessive or contextually marked. Evaluation depends on relationship, situation, preference structure and cultural convention.

**Project consequence:** the humanizer must not automatically soften direct Serbian or treat concise disagreement as defective.

## 2. Agreement/disagreement is context-sensitive

The same surface form can be acceptable or unacceptable depending on whether agreement or disagreement is socially preferred in that interaction. The source therefore treats context as part of the phenomenon, not an optional modifier.

**Project consequence:** no word list or regex can classify politeness/directness reliably.

## 3. Transactional vs interactional meaning

An utterance carries both propositional/task content and interpersonal work. Serbian dialogue in this corpus often prioritizes pragmatic clarity more strongly; English dialogue often packages the same task with more face-management.

**Project consequence:** editing must preserve both layers. A rewrite that keeps facts but destroys stance, closeness or formality is lossy.

## 4. Discourse markers and hedges are functional

Particles, hesitation markers, stance frames, address forms and similar items can soften disagreement, signal commitment, manage turn-taking or encode relationship. They are not automatically filler.

**Project consequence:** preserve `pa`, `mislim`, `znaš`, vocatives, repetitions and hedges when they perform interactional work.

## 5. Familiarity and power are dynamic context parameters

`ti/vi`, names, titles, kinship terms and degree of mitigation interact with social distance and relative power. Power is not fixed across an entire relationship.

**Project consequence:** formality decisions need local context; they cannot be inferred from one pronoun or title in isolation.

## 6. Translation can preserve proposition while losing interaction

The subtitle analysis shows that English→Serbian translation can increase directness and remove interactional units while retaining the central message. This changes formality, tone and the spectrum of language functions.

**Project consequence:** English-shaped Serbian can be over-indirect or over-padded, but the inverse operation “delete all hedges/markers” is equally wrong.

## 7. Positive expressive resources matter

The source is not merely an inventory of things to delete. Directness, particles, address, emotional explanation, concise contradiction, repetition and solidarity markers can all be legitimate Serbian resources.

**Project consequence:** humanization must preserve purposeful Serbian interactional texture rather than normalize everything to neutral exposition.
