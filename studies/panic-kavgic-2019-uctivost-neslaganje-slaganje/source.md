# Source identity

- **Author:** Olga Panić Kavgić
- **Title:** *Učtivost pri neslaganju i slaganju u engleskom i srpskom jeziku*
- **Edition/series:** E-disertacija, knj. 18
- **Publisher:** Filozofski fakultet, Novi Sad
- **Year:** 2019
- **ISBN:** 978-86-6065-535-8
- **COBISS.SR-ID:** 329992967
- **Artifact studied:** user-supplied PDF, 420 PDF pages, searchable embedded text with page images
- **Origin:** monograph developed from the author's 2014 doctoral dissertation on verbally expressed politeness in English, Serbian and translated film dialogue.

## Access and copyright

The PDF contains an explicit copyright notice prohibiting reproduction without the author's written permission. This study therefore stores only bibliographic identity, paraphrased observations, source locations, project-authored examples and evaluation cases. Source dialogue examples are not copied into the repository.

## Scope

The study covers the complete supplied PDF. The empirical material is a qualitative corpus of dialogue from five US and five Serbian films plus Serbian subtitle translations of selected US films. The source itself repeatedly limits generalization because of corpus size, genre and the mediated nature of film dialogue.

## Project relevance

Primary evidence classes:

- `NATIVE_USAGE`: Serbian pragmatic realization of agreement/disagreement, directness, mitigation, discourse markers, address and context.
- `INTERFERENCE`: English→Serbian pragmatic transfer, especially indirectness, interactional packaging and subtitle loss.
- `EDITING`: preserving interactional meaning and matching formality/relationship context.

This source does **not** independently establish modern Serbian `NORM` and does not justify AI-authorship detection.
